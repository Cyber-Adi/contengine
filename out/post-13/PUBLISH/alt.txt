Slide 1: You can freeze all of these. Yes, seriously.
Slide 2: Wine →. Pour leftover wine into an ice cube tray. Freeze. Drop cubes into pasta sauce, risotto, or stew. Restaurant-level flavor from last Tuesday's bottle.
Slide 3: Fresh ginger →. Throw the whole root in the freezer. Grate it straight from frozen, it shreds easier frozen than fresh. No peeling needed.
Slide 4: Egg yolks →. Leftover yolks from a recipe? Whisk with a pinch of salt, pour into ice cube molds. Thaw later for custards, carbonara, or enriching sauces.
Slide 5: Overripe bananas →. Peel first, then freeze. Frozen bananas blend into smoothies better than fresh. They also replace ice cream if you blend them solo. That's it. That's the hack.
