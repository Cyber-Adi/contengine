Slide 1: One $6 rotisserie chicken. Three meals. Zero waste.
Slide 2: Night 1: Eat it. Carve the breasts and thighs. Serve with whatever you have, rice, salad, bread. This is the easy one. Save every bone and scrap.
Slide 3: Day 2: Shred the leftovers. Pull all remaining meat off the bones. Toss into a grain bowl with greens, pickled onion, and whatever sauce is in the fridge. Or make chicken salad. Or quesadillas.
Slide 4: Day 3: Bone broth. Carcass + water + an onion + 2 celery stalks + salt. Simmer 2, 4 hours (or 45 min in a pressure cooker). Strain. You now have 6, 8 cups of stock worth $8, 12 at the store.
