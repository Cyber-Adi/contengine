# Growth research, @getfond (2026-10-07)

Caveat: nearly all sources are third-party marketing blogs, not Instagram or TikTok. Instagram's own ranking-signals page is dated 2023. Treat numbers as directional.

## 1. Instagram ranking signals for carousels
- Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach as top signals; still repeated in 2026. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/
- Sends/reach of 1-2% is called healthy, 3%+ viral. UNVERIFIED blog benchmark. https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured
- Measured save rate for carousels is about 0.05% (Socialinsider), far below blog claims of 1.5-15%; denominators differ. Same link; https://carouselli.com/blog/instagram-carousel-engagement
- Limit is 20 slides. More slides do not help by themselves; evidence supports gains only up to about 10, and padding hurts completion. "8-10 optimal" is guide opinion. https://purpletrendai.com/blog/instagram-carousel-limit-2026
- Mosseri, Oct 2024: multiple media "going to mean more interactions". Skipped carousels re-serve starting at slide 2. Same purpletrend link.

## 2. Cadence, Trial Reels, repurposing
- Nano accounts average about 2 posts/week (Later 2025, secondhand); guides recommend 3-5; no hidden penalty for more. 2/week is viable, not optimal. https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram
- Move to 3/week after two stable weeks of metrics and a third brief with complete copy. Own heuristic, UNVERIFIED.
- Trial Reels go to non-followers only, results in about 24h, schedulable. Eligibility unclear (200 vs 1,000 followers): confirm in-app. Reels only, so carousels cannot be trialled. https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels/
- Under 50k followers Reels win reach, carousels win saves, so a Reel cut of each carousel is a discovery lever. https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured

## 3. Caption SEO, hashtags, alt text
- Hashtags capped at 5; Mosseri reportedly said they do not improve reach. Use 3-5 specific ones in the caption. https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-5-hashtag-limit-2026
- Keywords in name, bio, and caption first line; Google/Bing index public posts. https://www.outfame.com/blog/instagram-seo-2026-how-to-rank-in-search-keywords-captions-alt-text
- Alt text: write it for accessibility; a ranking effect is NOT confirmed (same link, checked against Instagram sources 1 Oct 2026).
- TikTok indexes slide text overlays for search. https://www.attentionclaw.com/blog/tiktok-slideshow-limits-and-specs

## 4. First hour
- Consensus: reply to every comment early with a follow-up question, pin 1-2 best comments, reshare to Stories. Quantified lifts (e.g. 34%) are anecdotal, UNVERIFIED. https://www.inro.social/blog/dos-and-donts-to-reach-new-audiences-on-instagram-organic-reach-guide

## 5. TikTok photo mode
- Photo-beats-video claims (6.2x views) come from a vendor's 3-creator test; do not rely on them. https://reelbase.io/blog/photo-mode-vs-video-case-study ; conflicting: https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktoks-photo-post-comeback
- Slides: guides say 5-10 (API max 35 images, 90-char title, 4,000-char description), 9:16. https://www.attentionclaw.com/blog/tiktok-slideshow-limits-and-specs
- Trending sound advised; effect UNVERIFIED.
- Frequency: guides suggest daily; 2/week is a floor, not a tuned number.
- TikTok's native scheduler is video-only; photo posts need a third-party tool via API. https://help.socialbee.com/hc/en-us/articles/38046904635287-How-to-post-a-carousel-on-TikTok

## 6. Metrics a loop can rely on
- IG per post: reach, likes, comments, saves, sends, profile visits, follows. Per-slide reach is NOT confirmed (sources hedge); do not build the loop on it. https://usevisuals.com/blog/instagram-insights-for-carousels
- Loop on sends/reach, saves/reach, follows and profile visits per post, always with reach as denominator.
- TikTok per post: views, likes, comments, shares, saves, traffic sources, new followers; no per-slide data found. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/tiktok-metrics/

## 7. Do this
1. Hold 2/week for 4 weeks, logging sends/reach and saves/reach; add a third post only after two stable weeks.
2. Keep carousels at 6-8 slides and end with a send prompt slide, never pad toward 20.
3. Caption line 1 is the target keyword phrase; 3-5 hashtags in the caption; write alt text anyway.
4. Stay online 60 minutes after posting: reply with a question, pin the best comment, reshare to Stories.
5. Cut one short Reel per carousel (keyword on screen in first 3 seconds) and trial it if eligible.
6. Post the same carousel on TikTok at 9:16, 5-8 slides, keyword in overlay, trending sound, via a third-party scheduler; judge photo vs video on your own data only.
