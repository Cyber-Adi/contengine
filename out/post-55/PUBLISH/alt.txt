Slide 1: The mist is not for the vegetables. It is for you.
Slide 2: Produce is sold by the pound. Mist is water. Water has weight.
Slide 3: The misters run on timers, not on need. Some chains pair them with a recorded thunder cue. Wet produce looks plumper, glossier, and fresher than dry produce, and shoppers reach for it.
Slide 4: That water evaporates in your fridge within a day. You paid for it at the register. It does not come back off the receipt.
Slide 5: Here is the part that costs you twice. Misting is not good for all types of produce. On the wrong items the added moisture speeds up spoilage instead of slowing it.
Slide 6: Misting helps: leafy greens, herbs, broccoli, celery, anything with an exposed cut surface. Misting hurts: berries, mushrooms, peppers, citrus, squash, anything with a skin that holds water against itself.
Slide 7: Three moves, none of them cost anything. Shake it off over the case before you weigh it. Dry the skinned items before they go in the fridge. Line the crisper drawer with a paper towel and change it when it is damp.
Slide 8: You are not buying fresher vegetables. You are buying wetter ones. Know what you brought home, and how long it actually has. Send this to whoever does the groceries.
