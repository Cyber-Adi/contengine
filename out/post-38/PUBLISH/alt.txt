Slide 1: Every time you chop vegetables, you throw away free broth.
Slide 2: The system: keep a gallon-size freezer bag in your freezer. Every time you cook, toss vegetable scraps into the bag. When the bag is full, usually 2, 3 weeks, you make broth.
Slide 3: Save these:. Onion skins and ends. Carrot peels and tops. Celery leaves and ends. Mushroom stems. Herb stems (parsley, thyme, cilantro). Garlic skins. Corn cobs. Leek tops.
Slide 4: Skip these:. Brassicas (broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower) make broth bitter. Beet scraps turn everything red. Potato peels make it starchy and cloudy. Anything moldy or rotten, freezing doesn't fix that.
Slide 5: Full bag + water + salt + 45 minutes simmering. Strain. That's 8 cups of broth worth $6, 10 at the store. Made from scraps you were going to throw away.
