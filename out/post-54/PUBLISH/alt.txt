Slide 1: Three of the best ingredients in your kitchen go down the drain every week.
Slide 2: Pasta water. The liquid in a can of chickpeas. Bacon fat. All three are ingredients. All three go in the sink.
Slide 3: Pasta water. The starch and salt suspended in it are what bind sauce to noodles. Reserve half a cup before you drain. Add it to the pan a splash at a time.
Slide 4: Chickpea liquid is called aquafaba. Three tablespoons replaces one egg white. It whips to stiff peaks. Meringue, mousse, mayonnaise, all of it works.
Slide 5: Bacon fat. Strain it through a coffee filter into a jar. It keeps three months in the fridge. It is cooking fat, and it is the reason restaurant potatoes taste like that.
Slide 6: You already paid for these. You have been throwing away the part that was free. Track what you have before it becomes something you pour out. Send this to whoever cooks.
