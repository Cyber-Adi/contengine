Slide 1: Milk, eggs, and bread are at the back of the store on purpose.
Slide 2: You came in for three things. The store forces you to walk past every aisle to reach them. Retailers call this "forced traversal", the longer your path, the more you buy.
Slide 3: The center aisles are the most profitable real estate in the building. That's where the impulse purchases live: snacks, sodas, chips, candy. You weren't planning to buy any of it.
Slide 4: The average shopper who "just needs milk" leaves with 3, 5 unplanned items. That's not weak willpower. That's architecture.
Slide 5: Even the checkout lane isn't neutral. Candy, gum, and magazines are placed where you wait with nothing to do. Hunger + boredom + proximity = purchase.
Slide 6: The store layout is a funnel. You are the conversion.
