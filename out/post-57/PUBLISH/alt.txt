Slide 1: You bought fresh herbs Sunday. They were slime by Wednesday. It was never the herbs. It was the drawer.
Slide 2: The crisper drawer is built for vegetables - sealed, still, humid. Cut herbs are not vegetables. They're cut stems. Sealed in a bag, they suffocate and rot in days.
Slide 3: SOFT HERBS - treat them like flowers. Basil, cilantro, parsley, mint, dill: trim the stems, stand them in an inch of water, loose bag over the top, into the fridge. They last 2-3 weeks.
Slide 4: HARD HERBS - treat them like delicate laundry. Rosemary, thyme, oregano, sage: wrap loosely in a slightly damp paper towel, then into a container or loose bag in the fridge. They last 2-3 weeks too.
Slide 5: One exception: basil hates the cold. Refrigerate basil and it blackens. Keep it on the counter in water, like a bouquet, away from direct sun.
Slide 6: Already wilting? Two rescues:. 1. Ice-water bath - limp greens and soft herbs perk back up in 15-30 minutes. 2. Blend the rest with olive oil, freeze in an ice-cube tray. Instant flavor bombs for months.
