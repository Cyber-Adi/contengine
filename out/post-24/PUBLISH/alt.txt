Slide 1: Your fruit is killing your vegetables. And you're putting them in the same drawer.
Slide 2: Ethylene gas. Apples, bananas, avocados, tomatoes, and peaches release a natural ripening gas as they age. It's invisible and odorless. It accelerates spoilage in everything around them.
Slide 3: What it destroys. Leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, cucumbers, and carrots are ethylene-sensitive. Stored next to ethylene producers, they wilt and yellow 2, 3x faster.
Slide 4: The crisper drawer fix. If your fridge has two drawers:. Drawer 1 (high humidity) = greens, herbs, cucumbers. Drawer 2 (low humidity) = fruits. Never mix them. One drawer of each.
Slide 5: The counter rule. Bananas on the counter ripen everything nearby. Keep them isolated. A banana hook isn't aesthetic, it's functional.
