# TikTok profile research for @getfond (Wave A2)

Dated 2026-10-07. Sources are search-result summaries (TikTok help/newsroom where reachable, else third-party). Anything not confirmed on a TikTok page is marked UNVERIFIED. Confirm each in the app's Edit profile and Settings screens before acting.

## 1. Account type for a 17-year-old
- Minimum age to register is 13. Linking to TikTok For Business requires the owner to be 18+; appeal needs photo ID with birth date ([TikTok Ads help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/troubleshooting-linking-tiktok-and-tiktok-for-business-accounts?lang=en)). Business verification and TikTok Shop are also 18+ (same source; [TikTok Shop age](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/age-requirement-for-tiktok-shop)).
- Whether the in-app "switch to Business" toggle is age-gated: no TikTok page found. UNVERIFIED. Do not rely on it; it would also lose the best sounds.
- Music: Business accounts are limited to the Commercial Music Library; personal/Creator accounts get the full library incl. trending sounds (IP policy effective 2025-04-26) ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-business-vs-personal/), [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-licensing-rules)). Third-party, UNVERIFIED on TikTok pages.
- Analytics: personal accounts get TikTok Studio analytics (365-day range, no export) once there is one public post; Business gets downloadable reports ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-business-vs-personal/); [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-analytics/)).
- Scheduling: TikTok Studio desktop scheduler 10 days ahead, one source says videos only, not carousels. Sources disagree on which account types may schedule ([BrandGhost vs Metricool, per Hootsuite summary](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-business-vs-personal/)). UNVERIFIED. Keep phone-app posting.

## 2. Username vs name
- Username (@handle): 2-24 chars, letters/numbers/underscore/period, change once per 30 days (third-party: [HandleGrab](https://www.handlegrab.com/tiktok/how-to-change-tiktok-username)). Some TikTok Discover pages cite 7 days; treat the in-app message as truth. UNVERIFIED.
- Display name: up to 30 chars, change roughly every 7 days ([ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/how-to-change-tiktok-username-display-name)). UNVERIFIED which field carries which limit.
- Searchability and keyword weighting of name field: no authoritative source found. UNVERIFIED. Practical: handle "getfond" for brand, display name "fond | food waste tips" style keywords.

## 3. Bio
- 80 characters incl. spaces; line breaks count, emoji count two ([Replug](https://replug.io/blog/tiktok-bio-character-limit), [TypeCount](https://typecount.com/blog/tiktok-caption-character-limit)). Third-party, UNVERIFIED officially.
- Structure: what you get + for whom + soft CTA. Search keywords come from name and bio; TikTok ranking use of bio text is UNVERIFIED.

Older source says only one of Instagram or YouTube shows ([Replug](https://replug.io/blog/tiktok-bio-character-limit)). UNVERIFIED.

## 6. Settings affecting reach (ages 16-17)
- Account is private by default; switch to Public or the For You feed cannot reach non-followers. Per TikTok, private means no Duet, Stitch, downloads ([TikTok teen settings](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/account-privacy-settings/privacy-and-safety-settings-for-users-under-age-18?lang=en)).
- Public: Duet and Stitch default Friends; downloads off by default; DMs default No one; comments on private default Followers; "Suggest your account to others" off for 13-17 and never shown to adults even if on (same source and [Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/furthering-our-safety-and-privacy-commitments-for-teens-on-tiktok-us?lang=en)).
- LIVE hosting not permitted under 18; DMs only from 16; no gifts or Shop ([TikTok youth safety](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/protecting-teens)).
- For You: the under-16 exclusion is stated; no stated limit for 16-17 ([Guardian's Guide](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/tools-and-guides/guardians-guide)). Reach implication of "suggest" being off: UNVERIFIED.
- Comment filters: no source retrieved. UNVERIFIED.

## Recommendations
1. Stay on a personal (or Creator) account; do not seek Business until Adi is 18. Keeps the full music library.
2. Set the account to Public on day one; check it is not still private by default.
3. Handle "getfond"; display name with one keyword, e.g. "fond | food waste". Confirm change limits in-app.
4. Bio under 80 chars, no URL, no waitlist: what it teaches, who for, "something's coming".
5. Add the Instagram button if the app shows it; otherwise bio mention "also on IG @getfond".
6. Put slide 1 (hook) as cover; use the 90-char title as the keyword hook, caption for the CTA line.
7. Pin 3 evergreen save-magnets once 10+ posts exist.
8. Expect no LIVE, no DM growth, no suggest-to-others; plan reach purely via search and For You.
