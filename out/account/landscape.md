## 2. Patterns
- Name field (30 chars, the main IG search lever): keyword first, one keyword only, never a repeat of the handle. Template: Brand | Keyword. For fond: "fond | Food Waste Tips" or "fond | Grocery Savings" (pick one, do not stack).
- Bio (150 chars): who it is for plus promise, one proof point, one CTA with an arrow, one link. Faceless bios describe the account's value, not a person. Olio, TGTG and NoWaste skip audience and proof; that is the opening.
- Pre-launch constraint: proof must come from gtm.json verified stats ($2,913/yr, EPA), never user counts; CTA is waitlist only.
- Profile picture: faceless brands use a simple logo or niche icon that reads at small size and matches the grid. Consistent with the single-mark approach in tokens.json.

## 3. Audience language
Verbatim from the Notion Language Bank (20 rows, read-only). Sources are X, Quora, Anandtech and Lemmy, not Reddit.
1. "the moldy orange I found in the back of my fridge"
2. "cleaning out your fridge is such a humiliating experience. you bought these plums and you didn't eat them in time. you absolute failure."
3. "I just cleaned out a moldy fridge. Am I an adult now?"
4. "I keep buying groceries, buying take-out every day, and throwing the groceries away"
5. "The milk has a Nov 19 "sell by" date. Do I drink it?"
6. "Sour milk...I didn't even notice"
7. "I find that anything I put in the crisper drawer will always go bad because I forget to look in there"
8. "I seem to overestimate how much I need and just buy what I like"
9. "I'm just one person living alone and I manage to waste a lot of groceries."
10. "What's the item from the grocery store you throw away the most of?"
11. "Is 'best by' an expiration date? ... does that mean it expired?"
12. "If I have unopened tofu that expired about a month ago, is it still safe to eat?"
13. "Why did my avocados go from unripe to rotten without ever ripening so they're never edible?"
14. "I Feel Kind of Bad About Food Waste"
15. "How long after eggs expire can you still eat them?"

Words they use: moldy, back of my fridge, humiliating, failure, "still safe to eat", expired, "forget to look in there", "feel kind of bad". Words they never use (per the log): inventory, track, spoilage, reduce, environmental impact, portion control. Caveat from the log: no verbatim source frames the problem in dollars; the $2,913 money angle is unvalidated by user language and should be treated as a hypothesis to test.

## 4. Positioning gaps @getfond can own
1. Faceless, carousel-first, grocery-payer-focused: existing faceless accounts are zero-waste-moral; money-first accounts are on-camera haul creators.
2. Shame to relief: the audience speaks in guilt and self-mockery; competitors speak in efficiency. Lead with the fridge-cleanout moment, not the system.
3. Date-label confusion: deepest evidenced pain cluster (most Language Bank rows) and a proven TGTG tip type; own it with sourced answers (43% label stat is verified in gtm.json).
4. The overbuyer who "buys what I like, not what I need", including single-person households.
5. Honest pre-launch voice: competitors push app-store downloads; a no-claims, value-first account with a waitlist CTA is differentiated and within guardrails.
