# @getfond Instagram profile research (2026-10-07)

Caveat: Instagram's own pages mostly were not reachable; sources are marketing blogs, so treat as directional. UNVERIFIED marks single-source or conflicting claims.

## 1. Name field vs username
- Both are searchable; the Name field (30 chars) carries the keyword weight, the username (30 chars) is identity. https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-change-your-name-on-instagram/ , https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-handle-complete-guide
- Pattern: "Brand | what it does", e.g. "fond | Food Waste Tips".
- Name changes: most 2026 guides say twice per 14 days; Kicksta says the reverse (UNVERIFIED). Username has no published cap, repeated changes can trigger a temporary block, old handle held about 14 days, search visibility dips a few days. https://howsociable.com/guides/how-to-change-your-instagram-name

## 2. Bio
- 150 chars including spaces and line breaks; bio text is indexed for search. https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/instagram-seo , https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/instagram-bio-optimization-2026
- Structure: line 1 niche keyword plus who it is for; line 2 what followers get or rhythm; line 3 one action. Two or three natural keywords. Plain beats poetic for search. https://captions.ai/blog/instagram-bio-ideas
- Emoji: guides use them as bullets, each costs two chars. Brand rules forbid emojis, so noted only, not recommended.
- Honesty: no user counts, no waitlist or link wording (gtm.json honesty; site not live).

## 3. Links
- Any account can add up to 5 links (Edit profile, Links; phone app only), no follower minimum; only the first shows prominently. https://www.soci.ai/knowledge-articles/instagram-link-in-bio/
- No evidence an empty link field hurts (absence of evidence; UNVERIFIED). Mosseri said saying "link in bio" does not affect reach. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-doesnt-penalize-posts-that-include-link-in-bio/753899/
- No confirmed penalty for a tiktok.com link. A Linktree-style page adds a tool with no destination behind it. Do not add getfond.app until live.

## 4. Profile picture
- Upload square, 720px or larger PNG; shown near 110 px in a circle; keep the mark in the centre 60-70%. https://stan.store/blog/instagram-post-size-guide-2026/ , https://expertphotography.com/instagram-profile-picture-size-guide
- Simple mark or single letter beats a wordmark; fine text fails at that size. https://www.freelogoservices.com/blog/instagram-logo-size-guide/
- Contrast: Off-White mark on Slate Black, or Slate Black mark on Fond Green. Avoid Harvest Gold on Off-White (2.01:1 per CLAUDE.md); never Signal Red (loss figures only). Check on light and dark app themes.

## 5. Account type
- Everyone under 18 is placed in Teen Accounts; professional accounts must be public; under 16 needs parent permission to go public, 16-17 does not unless supervised. https://help.instagram.com/995996839195964 , https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/updates-to-teen-accounts/
- Creator has the full music library; Business gets royalty-free only. Both have Insights, contact buttons, native scheduling; neither is favoured in reach. https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account , https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account/
- Insights or scheduling differences for teen professional accounts: not found, UNVERIFIED; check the help page.
- Category: a neutral label such as "Education" or "Digital creator". Contact buttons: skip until a real inbox exists (judgement).

## 6. Pinned posts (max 3)
- Last pinned shows first (left); a 4th pin either auto-unpins or errors (sources disagree). https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/07/instagram-pin-posts-to-your-profile/ , https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/instagram-marketing-how-to-pin-posts-and-reels-to-a-profile/
- New account: an intro carousel (what this account is), the strongest value post, a save-worthy reference post. Rotate to top-saved posts after 2-4 weeks of Insights, then monthly.
- Check cover crops in the grid.

## 7. Story Highlights
- 2026 agency guides say they still convert profile visitors; all are marketing blogs, UNVERIFIED. https://socialbyidm.com/instagram-highlights-covers-names-a-strategy-that-converts-in-2026/ , https://www.digitalmarketingagency.sg/blog/instagram-story-highlights
- 3-5 max: Start here, Fridge storage, Grocery money, Food waste. A highlight needs real Stories first.
- Covers: 1080x1920, centre 60% safe zone, about 110 px shown, one icon style on Slate Black or Off-White, short keyword names. No manual reorder; newest-updated sorts first.

## 8. Discoverability settings
- "Show account suggestions on profiles" is two-way; leave on. https://studiomovellan.com/blog/how-to-get-your-instagram-account-suggested-to-others
- Search-engine indexing: one source says only professional accounts owned by over-18s are indexed (UNVERIFIED); leave any toggle on.
- Hidden Words: keep default offensive-comment filter, add custom spam phrases. https://help.instagram.com/700284123459336/
- Tags/mentions: allow everyone, tighten if spam appears (menu path UNVERIFIED).
- Threads: badge appears on the profile after joining Threads; hide via Edit profile toggle. https://contentstudio.io/blog/how-to-hide-threads-badge-on-your-instagram-profile

## Recommendations
1. Name field "fond | Food Waste Tips", username @getfond; set once, then leave.
2. Three-line bio, no emoji: niche keyword plus audience; what posts give; soft CTA matching gtm.json ctas.setup. No waitlist or link wording.
3. Leave the link empty until the TikTok has posts or getfond.app is live; then use one link.
4. Avatar: simple fond mark, Off-White on Slate Black (or Slate Black on Fond Green), centre 60-70%, 720px PNG, no text.
5. Switch to Creator (public), after confirming age and parent-consent rules and Insights on the help page.
6. Pin three: what-this-is, best value post, most save-worthy reference; rotate by saves after 2-4 weeks.
7. Highlights: 3-4 only, built from real Stories, matching icon covers.
8. Leave account suggestions on, keep default comment filters plus a custom spam list, hide the Threads badge unless using Threads.
