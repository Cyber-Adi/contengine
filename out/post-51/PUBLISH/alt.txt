Slide 2: "Manager's Special" stickers appear when a product is within 24-48 hours of its sell-by date. The store marks it down to move it before it goes unsellable - not unsafe. The markdown is typically 30-50%.
Slide 3: When they appear:. Early morning (7-9 AM) when night-crew restocks and dates are checked. And Sunday evening (5-8 PM) when stores pull product before Monday's delivery. Hit both windows and you cover the full markdown cycle.
