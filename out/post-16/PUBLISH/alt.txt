Slide 1: "There's nothing to eat" is almost always a lie. Here's the technique that proves it.
Slide 2: Open your fridge. Pull out every vegetable that's about to turn. Any combination works: bell peppers, zucchini, broccoli, sweet potato, onion, carrots, mushrooms, literally anything.
Slide 3: Chop it roughly. Toss with olive oil + salt + whatever spices you have. Spread on a sheet pan. 400°F. 25 minutes. Walk away.
Slide 4: Add a protein if you have one:. chicken thighs, sausage, tofu, canned chickpeas. All of them work. None of them are required.
Slide 5: This technique turns "I have nothing" into dinner in 30 minutes. The only ingredient is whatever's about to die in your fridge.
