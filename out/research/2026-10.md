Run date 2026-10-21. Read-only. Reddit and Quora were not reachable by the search tool (reddit.com blocked; Quora returned nothing), so every Reddit claim is UNVERIFIED and none appear here. Evidence types: C = complaint, A = ask, S = existing spend. Quotes are search-result text, not re-fetched pages; treat as snippet-level.

## Top 8 pains, ranked by evidence strength

1. **Leftovers and fridge items forgotten, then thrown out** (C, strong; 2,000-adult survey). Frigidaire/Atomik, Mar 2026: 74% throw out leftovers at least weekly; 90% chose takeout despite a good meal in the fridge. Quote: "knowing what to do with them" (snippet). https://www.electroluxgroup.com/en/news/new-frigidaire-survey-reveals-good-intentions-arent-enough-americans-still-toss-their-leftovers/
2. **Grocery prices up; waste falls when people plan** (C+S, strong; n=1,000). ReFED/YouGov Jan 2026: 58% say spend rose; checking what is at home before shopping helped. https://www.biocycle.net/refed-report-affordability-household-food-waste/
3. **Produce, bread, greens spoil first** (C, strong). NetCredit/Censuswide: bread 22.7% tossed, lettuce 22.6%; ReFED: produce is 45.4% of surplus. https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/survey-finds-the-10-most-wasted-grocery-items-in-the-u-s/
4. **Impulse and recipe-sized overbuying (herbs, bulk potatoes)** (C, medium; editorial, not user voice). https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/articles/14-most-wasted-groceries-americans-140023591.html
5. **Inventory apps die from data-entry friction** (C, medium; app-store snippets and competitor blogs, biased). My Pantry Tracker review: non-food items fill the "expired" list unless dates are hand-edited. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-pantry-tracker/id1567647197?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone
6. **Wrong or missing expiry data** (C, medium). NoWaste reviewer: "I can even understand that it wouldn't have the correct expiration for every item, but can't some items be correct!?" Also lost inventory after an update. https://justuseapp.com/en/app/926211004/nowaste-food-inventory-list/reviews
7. **Reminders paywalled** (C+S, weak-medium). NoWaste reminders are Pro only ($6.99/yr per its site); a reviewer bought Pro for expiry alerts. https://www.nowasteapp.com/home/
8. **Surplus-bag apps: unpredictable contents, phantom bags** (C, medium; Trustpilot 9,793 reviews, mixed). Stale items, no allergen filter, bag gone during payment. A different job than fond's. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/toogoodtogo.com

## Pillars and angles: support and contradiction

- **takeout-guilt** (posts 7, 19, 52): strongly supported (74% weekly toss, 90% takeout over fridge). Strongest angle this month.
- **money-leak** (2, 5, 8, 32, 40): supported by the price-pressure finding; affordability is the motive, not guilt.
- **date-label-lie** (6, 56): supported (labels read as strict deadlines), second tier.
- **Store It Right and save-magnets** (58, 43, 9, 61, 57): supported by "produce and bread spoil first" and "out of sight".
- Contradiction: household waste is falling (ReFED, first drop since 2020). Avoid "you are wasting more" trend framing.
- No evidence either way for Supermarket Secrets (pricing psychology); Reddit unchecked, so this is absence, not contradiction.
- All figures here are survey aggregations: render qualitatively per CLAUDE.md, never as hero type.

## Product signals for fond

People ask or pay for: near-zero data entry (receipt photo, auto dates); free expiry reminders; "what do I cook with this" for items about to expire; household sharing; partial-use handling; data safety after updates. Apps that need upkeep or paywall reminders draw the complaints. Kitche and the original Fridgely are reported gone from stores (competitor blog, UNVERIFIED), so the niche looks open.
