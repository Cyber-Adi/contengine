Slide 1: Those brown bananas on your counter are worth $8. Stop throwing them away.
Slide 2: Smoothie base. Peel, freeze in a bag. One frozen banana replaces ice AND adds sweetness to any smoothie. A bag of 4 frozen bananas lasts a month and saves you from buying smoothie mix ($6, 8/bag).
Slide 3: Pancake binder. Mash one banana + 2 eggs + pinch of cinnamon. That's the entire batter. No flour needed. 3-ingredient pancakes in 10 minutes. Kids don't know the difference.
Slide 4: Oatmeal sweetener. Mash half a banana into your oatmeal instead of sugar, honey, or maple syrup. It dissolves into the heat and sweetens naturally. You just saved a squeeze of $9 maple syrup.
