Slide 1: You're throwing away ingredients worth more than what you cooked.
Slide 2: Parmesan rinds →. Don't throw the rind away. Drop it into soups, stews, or risotto while it simmers. It melts into the broth and adds depth you can't get from anything else. One rind = $3, 4 of flavor.
Slide 3: Pasta water →. That starchy water is a free emulsifier. A ladle of it turns any sauce silky. Every Italian restaurant does this. Every home cook pours it down the drain.
Slide 4: Chickpea can liquid (aquafaba) →. The liquid in your can of chickpeas whips like egg whites. Meringue, mousse, cocktail foam, all from something you were about to rinse off.
Slide 5: Bacon grease →. Strain it into a jar. Store in the fridge. Use it instead of butter or oil for roasting vegetables, frying eggs, or making cornbread. Your grandparents did this. It's not gross. It's free cooking fat.
