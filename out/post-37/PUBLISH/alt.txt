Slide 1: Your freezer doesn't store food. It pauses time.
Slide 2: Most people use their freezer for ice cream, frozen pizza, and meat. It can hold almost everything in your kitchen, . if you freeze it at the right time.
Slide 3: Bread. Freezes perfectly for 3, 6 months. Slice before freezing, pull individual slices straight to the toaster. No thawing needed. This alone saves most households $3, 5/week in stale bread waste.
Slide 4: Cheese. Hard and semi-hard cheeses (cheddar, mozzarella, parmesan) freeze for 6+ months. Shred before freezing, frozen blocks crumble when thawed. Shredded cheese goes straight from freezer to pan.
Slide 5: Cooked rice and grains. Cool completely. Freeze flat in bags. Reheat with a splash of water. Lasts 4, 6 months. Frozen rice actually makes BETTER fried rice than fresh, the texture is dryer and crisps up faster.
Slide 6: Fresh herbs. Chop them. Mix with olive oil. Pour into ice cube trays. Freeze. Drop a cube into any pan and you have instant herb butter. One tray lasts 2 months.
Slide 7: The rule: Freeze food BEFORE it expires, not after. Freezing doesn't fix food that's already gone bad. It suspends food that's still good. Time the freeze, and you never throw that item away again.
