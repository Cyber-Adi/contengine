Slide 1: Your freezer is a pause button. You're using it for ice and not much else. Here are 5 things you didn't know you could freeze.
Slide 2: 1. WINE. Leftover wine -> freeze in an ice-cube tray. Drop a cube straight into sauces, braises, and pan deglazes. Never pour the last of the bottle out again.
Slide 3: 2. FRESH GINGER. Freeze the whole knob. Grate it straight from frozen - no peeling, no stringy mess, and it lasts for months instead of shriveling in the drawer.
Slide 4: 3. EGGS. Not in the shell - crack them first. Whisk, pour into a tray, freeze. Perfect for baking and scrambles. Yolks and whites freeze separately too.
Slide 5: 4. GREEK YOGURT. About to expire? Freeze dollops on a tray. Drop them frozen into smoothies, or thaw for baking. Texture shifts slightly - flavor doesn't. 5. FRESH HERBS. Chop, pack into a tray, top with olive oil, freeze. Sauté-ready flavor cubes that last for months.
Slide 6: The freezer doesn't stop time. But it buys you weeks you were throwing away. We built an app that tracks what's about to expire - so you can freeze it in time. Save it before your fridge does the forgetting. Follow for more, something's on the way.
