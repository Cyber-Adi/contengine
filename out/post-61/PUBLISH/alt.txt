Slide 1: Your fridge came with a place to put everything. Most of those places are wrong.
Slide 2: The egg holder is built into your door. That's the worst spot in the whole fridge for eggs.
Slide 3: The door opens dozens of times a day. Every open lets warm kitchen air in. The door runs 7-10°F warmer than the back of the fridge - closer to room temperature than to "cold storage.".
Slide 4: Bottom shelf, back: the coldest zone in the fridge - this is where raw meat and fish belong. It also keeps any drips contained below everything else, instead of dripping onto food you won't cook first.
Slide 5: Middle shelf: dairy, eggs, leftovers. Consistent mid-range cold, easy to see, easy to rotate. Top shelf: the least perishable stuff - drinks, ready-to-eat items, leftovers you'll finish today.
Slide 6: Crisper drawers aren't interchangeable. High-humidity setting = leafy greens and herbs (they wilt when they lose moisture). Low-humidity setting = fruit (it rots faster in trapped moisture). Same drawer shape, opposite jobs.
Slide 7: The fridge isn't one temperature. It's five zones. You've been using one.
