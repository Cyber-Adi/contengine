# SECTION 1 · THE fond DESIGN SYSTEM

*Copy this section verbatim into any design tool along with the slide specs. Hex codes, fonts, and layout rules are non-negotiable.*

## 1.1 Brand character

fond's faceless account looks like **investigative journalism about your kitchen**: the authority of a financial broadsheet, the warmth of a well-lit pantry. It is editorial, not lifestyle. Every slide is information design, a diagram, a number, a comparison, or a reference card. Never decoration. If a slide could appear in a food blogger's feed, it is wrong. If it could appear in a print explainer from The Economist about grocery stores, it is right.

## 1.2 Color palette (exact hex, no substitutions)

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Slate Black** | `#14161A` | Dark slide backgrounds. Near-black with a cool undertone, never pure `#000`. |
| **Off-White** | `#F5F2EC` | Light slide backgrounds and primary text on dark. Warm paper tone, never pure `#FFF`. |
| **fond Green** | `#2E5E4E` | The brand color. CTA cards, the word "fond," positive and solution states, green checkmarks. |
| **Steel Blue** | `#4A7BA6` | Secondary accent. Safe and good data states, key terms in reframes, CTA text on dark. |
| **Signal Red** | `#D64541` | Loss, waste, and cost ONLY. Never decorative. If it is not a negative number or a warning, it is not red. |
| **Harvest Gold** | `#D9A441` | Warm highlight. Sparingly: one emotional word per carousel maximum, thread elements, caution data states. |
| **Ink Gray** | `#6B7280` | Captions, source citations, secondary labels. |

Maximum 3 accent colors per slide. Backgrounds are always Slate Black or Off-White. No gradients, no photos as backgrounds, no textures except the parchment tone reserved for Origin Story posts.

## 1.3 Typography

| Font | Weight | Role | Size @ 1080×1350 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Playfair Display** | Bold | Hooks (slides 1 to 2) and reframes (slides 7 to 8). The editorial voice. | 72–96pt hooks, 56–72pt reframes |
| **DM Sans** | Regular / Medium | All body copy, labels, CTAs. | 32–40pt body, 28pt labels |
| **Space Grotesk** | Bold | Every number, stat, dollar amount, percentage. Numbers are never set in the body font. | 80–140pt hero numbers, matches body size inline |
| **DM Sans** | Regular, Ink Gray | Source citations, bottom of stat slides. | 20–22pt |

Line length no more than 8 words per line on hooks. Body copy no more than 40 words per slide, ragged-left, never justified, never centered except hero numbers and CTA cards.

## 1.4 Layout grid

- Canvas: **1080 × 1350 px** (4:5 portrait), export PNG, sRGB.
- Margins: 96px all sides. Nothing but the continuous thread may touch the margin zone.
- Handle "@" placement: bottom-left corner inside margin, DM Sans 24pt, Ink Gray, every slide.
- Slide-position counter ("3/8"): bottom-right, same spec.
- The continuous thread occupies a consistent zone (bottom 15%, or one side rail) and never overlaps text.

## 1.5 Illustration and iconography style

- **Flat vector illustration only.** 2px consistent stroke or filled shapes. No 3D, no drop shadows, no gradients, no skeuomorphism, no photorealistic AI renders.
- Food items are drawn as simplified, recognizable silhouettes with one accent color plus Off-White or Slate line work.
- Icons: geometric, single-weight, from one visual family per carousel. All outlined or all filled, never mixed.
- Diagrams are the hero: timelines, flow loops, bar comparisons, floor plans, split-frames, before-and-afters, pie-on-a-plate. Every diagram must be readable in under 2 seconds at phone size.
- **Metaphor objects** (receipts, boarding passes, police reports, textbook covers, price tags, scale readouts) are the signature move for reframing data. Use one per carousel where the script calls for it.

## 1.6 The continuous thread (non-negotiable)

Every carousel has one design element that progresses across all slides and completes on the final slide. Thread stays at 15 to 20 percent of slide area maximum, never competes with text, and its completion coincides with the fond CTA. If the thread is a line, it must exit the right edge of each slide and re-enter the next slide's left edge at the identical vertical position (the "follow the line" mechanic).

## 1.7 The fond CTA card (final slide, standardized)

- Background: fond Green `#2E5E4E`.
- "fond" in lowercase, DM Sans Medium, Off-White, centered. The only time the wordmark appears.
- One line of CTA copy (Playfair for the reframe fragment, DM Sans for the ask).
- One send-trigger line in Off-White at 70 percent opacity.
- "Join the waitlist, link in bio" plus a small down-arrow glyph. No logo, no app screenshots until launch, no QR codes.

## 1.8 Never do

- Stock photography or photorealistic people, kitchens, or lifestyle imagery
- Gradients, drop shadows, bevels, glassmorphism
- Pure black or pure white backgrounds
- Signal Red used decoratively
- More than 40 words of body copy on a slide
- Centered paragraph text
- The same visual layout on two consecutive slides (every swipe must feel like arrival somewhere new)
- "PantryPal." The product is **fond**.

# SECTION 7 · DESIGN PRINCIPLES GOVERNING THIS BATCH

1. **Slide 1 contains a visual contradiction, mystery, or discomfort.** The viewer sees something that does not match expectation. That is the swipe trigger.
2. **Slide 2 is a second hook, never a continuation.** Instagram re-serves carousels 24 to 48 hours later showing slide 2 first. It must stop a cold viewer on its own.
3. **Interior slides use diagrams, comparisons, timelines, and spatial layouts.** The visual should teach even if the words are unreadable.
4. **One slide per carousel is the evidence slide**, formatted like a receipt, report, blueprint, status readout, or data table. This is what makes the account read as journalism rather than opinion.
5. **The dark-background slide is the emotional escalation point**, the biggest number or the hardest truth.
6. **The final slide returns to simplicity.** Pure type, generous whitespace, one powerful sentence, then the CTA card. After the visual density of the middle, restraint reads as a closing argument.
7. **Every carousel carries a send-trigger line** on the CTA card. DM shares are weighted far more heavily than likes, and sends are the growth lever.
8. **Signal Red appears only on loss, waste, and cost.** If it is not a negative number or a warning, it is not red.
9. **Nothing in this batch requires photography, 3D rendering, or an app screenshot.** fond is pre-launch. Do not mock up a product interface.
