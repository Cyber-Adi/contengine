## SECTION 1 — BRAND SYSTEM (Recap, self-contained)

**Brand character:** fond's faceless account reads like investigative journalism about your kitchen — the authority of a financial broadsheet, the warmth of a well-lit pantry. Editorial, not lifestyle. Every slide is information design: a diagram, a number, a comparison. Never decoration.

**Color palette (exact hex, no substitutions):**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Slate Black | `#14161A` | Dark slide backgrounds |
| Off-White | `#F5F2EC` | Light backgrounds + primary text on dark |
| fond Green | `#2E5E4E` | CTA cards, the wordmark, solution states |
| Steel Blue | `#4A7BA6` | Safe/good data states, key reframe terms |
| Signal Red | `#D64541` | Loss, waste, cost ONLY — never decorative |
| Harvest Gold | `#D9A441` | Sparing emotional highlight, one per carousel max |
| Ink Gray | `#6B7280` | Captions, source citations |

**Typography:**

| Font | Role | Size @ 1080×1350 |
|---|---|---|
| Playfair Display Bold | Hooks (slide 1–2), reframes (final slides) | 72–96pt hooks, 56–72pt reframes |
| DM Sans Regular/Medium | Body, labels, CTAs | 32–40pt body, 28pt labels |
| Space Grotesk Bold | Every number, stat, dollar amount | 80–140pt hero numbers |
| DM Sans Regular, Ink Gray | Source citations | 20–22pt |

**Layout:** Canvas 1080×1350px (4:5), PNG, sRGB. Margins 96px all sides — only the thread may enter the margin zone. Handle bottom-left, DM Sans 24pt, Ink Gray. Slide counter "n/8" bottom-right, same spec.

**Illustration style:** Flat vector only, 2px consistent stroke or filled shapes. No 3D, gradients, drop shadows, photorealism, stock photography. Diagrams are the hero — timelines, flow loops, bar comparisons, split-frames. Metaphor objects (receipts, boarding passes) are the signature reframing move.

**The continuous thread (non-negotiable):** every carousel has one design element that progresses across all 8 slides and completes on the final slide. ≤15–20% of slide area, never overlapping text.

**The fond CTA card (final slide, standardized):** fond Green `#2E5E4E` background. "fond" lowercase, DM Sans Medium, Off-White, centered. One reframe line (Playfair) + one ask line (DM Sans). One send/save-trigger line in Off-White 70%. "Join the waitlist — link in bio ↓." No logo, no screenshots, no QR codes.

**Never do:** stock photography, gradients/shadows/glassmorphism, pure black/white backgrounds, decorative Signal Red, >40 words body copy per slide, centered paragraph text, repeated layout on consecutive slides, "PantryPal" anywhere — the product is **fond**.

### CAROUSEL #45 — "The Color of Fresh Is a Lie"

**Slide 1 — DARK BG `#14161A`**
> That apple looks fresh

- *Visual:* A single illustrated apple with an exaggerated, uncanny shine, a small spray-mist icon beside it. Too perfect — the visual primes suspicion before any explanation.
- *Thread:* "FRESH" stamp, top-right corner, crisp and bold, Signal Red outline.

**Slide 2 — DARK BG `#14161A` (backup hook — must land cold)**
> You have never once seen this store's food
> in its actual condition. Only its optimized one.

- *Thread:* Stamp's edges begin to blur slightly.

**Slide 3 — LIGHT BG `#F5F2EC`**
> Produce misting systems spray a fine water mist every few minutes. It makes vegetables look dewy and fresh. It also adds water weight — you're paying per pound for water — and accelerates rot by introducing moisture to surfaces.

- *Visual:* Two-part diagram: mist landing on lettuce, labeled "looks fresh" (green check) + "adds weight to your bill" (Signal Red) + "accelerates spoilage" (Signal Red). Two bad outcomes hidden behind one good appearance.
- *Thread:* Stamp ink starts to fade at the corners.

**Slide 4 — LIGHT BG `#F5F2EC`**

**Slide 5 — DARK BG `#14161A`**
> Meat cases use specific lighting. Red-spectrum lights make beef look redder and fresher. Under neutral light, the same steak looks brown and dull. The meat hasn't changed. The light has.

- *Visual:* Split image of the same steak — left under warm red store lighting (vibrant), right under neutral white light (duller, older). Identical product, two different apparent ages. The most visceral slide in the carousel.
- *Thread:* Stamp now half-faded, one word barely readable.

**Slide 6 — LIGHT BG `#F5F2EC`**
> Bakery items labeled "baked fresh today" may have arrived frozen and been reheated on-site. The legal definition of "baked" includes finishing a par-baked product. Fresh is a feeling, not a fact.

- *Thread:* Stamp nearly gone, cracked through the middle.

**Slide 7 — LIGHT BG `#F5F2EC` (the reframe)**

- *Visual:* The word "FRESH" in large Playfair Display, letters visibly cracked and peeling like old paint — the deterioration of the word mirrors the deterioration of the concept.

**Slide 8 — DARK BG `#14161A` (Final, CTA Tier 2)**

**Slide 8 — DARK BG `#14161A` (Final, CTA Tier 2)**

> fond helps you track what your household actually eats — so you serve what gets used.

- **Captions:** burned in always. Assume sound off.
- **Audio:** trending ambient or low-percussion audio verified in-app at posting time. No voiceover.

## SECTION 6 — DESIGN PRINCIPLES GOVERNING THIS BATCH

1. One idea per slide. Never two.
2. Every swipe must feel like arrival somewhere new — no repeated layouts on consecutive slides.
3. Slide 2 is always a second hook, not a continuation — it must stop a cold viewer who never saw slide 1. All three carousels in this batch got a newly written backup-hook slide to meet this v2 rule.
4. Numbers are always Space Grotesk Bold. Signal Red `#D64541` marks loss and cost only — never decoration. Steel Blue marks potential, savings, or "safe" states.
5. The thread never overlaps text and never exceeds 20% of slide area.
6. The second-to-last content slide is the screenshot/save slide — bordered, saveable reference-card treatment (Post 44 slide 7, Post 46 slide 7).
7. The final slide is always the standardized fond CTA card on fond Green `#2E5E4E`, with a send/save-trigger line.
8. Copy is verbatim — do not rewrite, paraphrase, or "improve" the sourced scripts.
9. The product is **fond**, lowercase, appearing only on the final slide (carousels) or the CTA card (Reel).
10. Kinetic Reel numbers never use illustrated icons — pure typographic motion only, per the established Reel vocabulary (The Number, Inflation Multiplier, Double Tax, $56 Friday). This batch's Reel follows that convention rather than importing the carousel's illustrated boarding-pass/car icons.
