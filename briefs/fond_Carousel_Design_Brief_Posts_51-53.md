## SECTION 1: BRAND SYSTEM

### Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Dark background | Slate Black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Light background | Off-White | `#F5F1E8` |
| Primary text (on dark) | Off-White | `#F5F1E8` |
| Primary text (on light) | Slate Black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Highlight / CTA / key words | Steel Blue | `#4A7FAC` |
| Waste / loss / negative data | Signal Red | `#D93A2B` |
| Emotional accent / warmth | Warm Gold | `#D4A843` |

**Rules:**
- Signal Red is used ONLY for waste, loss, or negative data. Never decoratively.
- Warm Gold is used for emotional beats, key reframe words, and the CTA callback element.
- Steel Blue is the app's voice — used on CTAs, reframe words, and featured terms.
- Never use gradient backgrounds or drop shadows.

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| Headline / reframe opener | Playfair Display | Bold |
| Body copy / explanations | DM Sans | Regular |
| CTA text | DM Sans | Medium |
| Hero stats / hero numbers | Space Grotesk | Bold |
| Slide labels / micro-copy | DM Sans | Regular |

**Rules:**
- Max 40 words of body copy per slide.
- Numbers and stats always use Space Grotesk Bold.
- Final slide of every carousel: Playfair Display for the reframe, DM Sans for the CTA.
- No italic text. No decorative scripts.

### Layout Rules

- Canvas: 1080 × 1080 px (square, Instagram native)
- Safe zone margin: 80px all sides
- Account handle: bottom-right corner, every slide — `@fondapp` in DM Sans Regular, 14–16pt, muted (70% opacity)
- Slide 1 is always DARK BG
- Final slide is always DARK BG
- Dark/light background alternation every 2–3 slides
- No lifestyle photography, no faces, no people cooking
- Illustration style: flat, editorial, diagrammatic — NOT food-blog aesthetic

## SECTION 2: CONTINUOUS VISUAL THREAD SYSTEM

Every carousel has a single design element that progresses across all slides and completes on the final slide. This drives swipe-through rate by creating visual momentum — each new slide feels like arriving somewhere, not just reading another frame.

**Thread rules:**
- Occupies a consistent position on every slide (same corner or edge)
- Progresses in one direction — fills, grows, advances, or transforms
- Maximum 15–20% of slide area
- Never competes with text — keep it below or beside the primary content zone
- Completes/resolves on the final slide, timed to the CTA

### CAROUSEL #53 — "Counter vs. Fridge: Stop Refrigerating These 8 Foods"

**Pillar:** Store It Right (Pillar 3)
**CTA Tier:** Tier 2
**Format:** Carousel · 7 slides
**Post Day:** Friday Week 18
**Post #:** 53

---

**Slide 1 — DARK BG**

> You're probably refrigerating half these foods.
> Refrigerating them is making them worse.
> And you've been doing it your whole life.

*Typography:* Playfair Display Bold, Off-White. Three lines, measured pacing. "Making them worse" in Warm Gold — not angry, just true.

*Visual:* A large, open refrigerator illustration (front view, door open). Inside the fridge, clearly arranged on the shelves: a tomato, an avocado, a banana, an onion, a garlic bulb, a potato, a loaf of bread, a lemon. Each item has a small Signal Red X badge in its corner — tiny, not aggressive. The fridge is full of things that shouldn't be there. The visual is immediately recognizable — every viewer has seen this exact fridge.

*Thread state:* Corner diagram: fridge icon with all 8 food icons packed inside. Counter is empty.

---

**Slide 2 — LIGHT BG**

> **The rule:** Cold air preserves food vulnerable to bacteria and moisture loss. But cold also breaks down starches in vegetables, stops ripening mid-track, and turns fats grainy. Not every food benefits. The question is always: is this food vulnerable, or is it alive?

*Typography:* DM Sans Regular, Slate Black. "Vulnerable or alive?" in Space Grotesk Bold, Steel Blue — the decision framework in 3 words.

*Visual:* Two-column layout, clean dividing line center. Left column (Steel Blue header): "Cold benefits" — bullet list of 4 items with green checks: raw meat, dairy, cut produce, opened condiments. Right column (Warm Gold header): "Cold harms" — bullet list of 4 items with Signal Red X: foods mid-ripening, high-starch vegetables, intact aromatics, bread. Each list uses DM Sans Regular, 14pt. The two-column rule is the decision framework — simple enough to remember.

*Thread state:* One icon migrates — tomato moves from fridge to counter in the corner diagram.

---

**Slide 3 — LIGHT BG**

> **Tomatoes:** Cold destroys the enzymes that create flavor. A refrigerated tomato tastes like a tomato-shaped nothing. Counter: 3–5 days ripe, full flavor. Fridge: 2 weeks, no flavor.
> **Avocados:** Cold stops ripening entirely. Counter until ripe, then fridge for 1–2 days max — any longer and the flesh browns and goes mushy.

*Typography:* DM Sans Regular, Slate Black. "Tomato-shaped nothing" in Space Grotesk Bold — the phrase is blunt enough to be memorable. Shelf-life numbers in Space Grotesk Bold, Steel Blue.

*Visual:* Two side-by-side comparison panels. Left panel: Tomato. **Slide 4 — DARK BG**

> **Onions & Garlic:** Cold and humidity make them sprout and go mushy. They need airflow and dry conditions — a counter bowl or mesh bag. Exception: cut onion → fridge, airtight container only.

*Typography:* DM Sans Regular, Off-White. "Exception" flagged in Warm Gold — signal that a rule has an asterisk.

## SECTION 6: DESIGN PRINCIPLES

**1. No lifestyle photography.** Every visual is illustrated, diagrammatic, or typographic. Stock images of kitchens, cooking people, or styled food are explicitly off-brand.

**2. Slide 1 is the scroll-stopper.** It carries the entire hook. The viewer makes a 0.5-second decision on Slide 1. The visual must communicate the premise without the copy.

**3. Every slide is a visual arrival.** The viewer should feel they've landed somewhere new on each swipe — different background tone, different visual element type, different information density. The swipe must feel like reward.

**4. Signal Red is earned.** It appears only when waste, loss, or deception is the subject. Using it decoratively destroys its impact.

**5. The cheat sheet slide earns saves.** Slide 6 of Post 53 is a save magnet — build it first, then design the other slides to earn it.

**6. The account name is "fond."** Lowercase. Not "PantryPal." Not "the app." CTAs say "fond" for Tier 2 and above.

**7. The final slide is the quietest.** After a carousel of data, the last slide earns its simplicity. No visual clutter. Just the reframe and the ask.

**8. Thread elements are subtle, not decorative.** The clock, the receipt, the icon-migration are small. They reward viewers who swipe through, not viewers who glance.
